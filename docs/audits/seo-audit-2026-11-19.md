# PisicaTa article SEO audit

Audit date 19 November 2026.

The audit covers the 229 current staging articles on revision `147822f82c185f9a1e12e543918157aaa3f80d51`, their category relationships, article HTML and cover manifest, plus the Laravel templates and routing in the local checkout. It includes a fresh crawl of all 229 Pi article URLs. All responded HTTP 200 with one H1, article text in the initial HTML, a self-canonical, and parseable Article and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD.

This is a publication-readiness and on-page audit. It does not include Search Console queries, impressions, rankings, backlinks, competitor research, a medical review of every claim, or measured Core Web Vitals. Counts of incoming links refer to contextual links in article bodies, excluding navigation, category cards and automatically related articles. Proposed topic consolidation is an editorial hypothesis, pending search-performance evidence. P1 means urgent editorial attention, P2 means substantive review, and P3 means routine improvement. These are review priorities, not Google scores.

## Opinion

The subject coverage, original URL inventory, server-rendered pages, and existing summaries give this publication a useful foundation. The most important work is to improve the reliability and completeness of the inherited content. Template metadata alone cannot make a sparse photo list useful or substantiate medical claims.

The application emits noindex headers on staging and the Caddy block also unconditionally emits them. Updating only `SITE_INDEXABLE` would therefore be insufficient if that same Caddy block were used publicly. Robots blocking is also configured on staging. A robots.txt disallow does not itself substitute for a noindex directive on a publicly reachable page.

## Urgent editorial examples

- **ID 95, poisoning first aid.** The body presents milk as a general first-aid response. This needs a veterinarian's review and replacement with authoritative emergency guidance. The Pet Poison Helpline explains why milk is not a universal antidote and home treatment should be professionally directed. [Poisoning misconceptions](https://www.petpoisonhelpline.com/uncategorized/five-pet-poisoning-first-aid-misconceptions/)
- **ID 78, scruffing as discipline.** The body recommends holding a cat by the scruff and pinning its head as an anti-aggression technique. Current International Cat Care handling guidance advises against scruffing and forceful restraint. [Cat-friendly care](https://icatcare.org/resources/cat-carer-guide-cat-friendly-veterinary-care.pdf)
- **ID 193, raw feeding.** The body says correct storage reduces Salmonella risk to zero and makes broad nutritional-completeness claims. Those claims need substantial correction with qualified veterinary review. AVMA discusses pathogen risks from raw diets. [AVMA raw-food guidance](https://avma.org/KB/Policies/Pages/Raw-or-Undercooked-Animal-Source-Protein-in-Cat-and-Dog-Diets.aspx)
- **IDs 167 and 170, visual lists.** The 40 sleeping-place examples are now a 64-word introduction. The 20-animal comparison article is now 43 words of labels. A single new cover does not deliver either title's promise. Restore meaningful visual examples and descriptions or revise each page's purpose and title.

The rebuild intentionally removed legacy images, but that choice left some image-led content incomplete. This is a defect in the rebuild's content handling that needs repair.

## Recommended order

1. Resolve unsafe or unsupported advice and image-dependent content gaps.
2. Add genuine authorship, reviewed sources, and an editorial review process.
3. Repair empty headings, unresolved links and redirected internal links. Improve the copy and snippets where useful.
4. Define topical hubs and differentiate overlapping content using search-performance data.
5. Prepare and verify production indexing, canonical origins, redirects and sitemap. Measure mobile performance after deployment.
6. Improve key-page imagery and Discover readiness without promising eligibility or traffic.

## Reference guidance

Google emphasizes useful, reliable content and accurate authorship. E-E-A-T is not a numerical score or a single ranking factor. Meta descriptions and titles can influence search presentation, but Google may choose its own snippets and title links. Article schema authorship and large images can improve how the page is understood or presented without guaranteeing a rich result.
